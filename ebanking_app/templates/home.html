System Overview & Capabilities

Welcome to the core administrative dashboard. This centralized system is designed to streamline daily financial operations, manage customer relationships, and track the complete lifecycle of loan products. Key capabilities include:

  • Customer & Account Management: Register new clients, capture KYC (Know Your Customer) details, and manage active savings or checking accounts.
  • Loan Origination: Process new loan applications, link guarantors, and manage approval statuses, requested amounts, and interest rates.
  • Repayment Tracking: Log individual loan payments and maintain real-time repayment summaries to track outstanding balances.
  • Transaction Processing: Record daily banking transactions, categorize them by type (e.g., deposits, withdrawals), and map them to customer accounts.
  • Staff Administration: Maintain internal records of bank personnel, their departmental assignments, and operational roles.
